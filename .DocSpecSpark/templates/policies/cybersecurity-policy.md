# {{COMPANY_NAME}} Cybersecurity Policy

## Purpose

This policy sets baseline cybersecurity controls for systems, data, users, and vendors supporting {{COMPANY_NAME}}.

## Control Areas

1. Asset inventory, ownership, and system accountability.
2. Access control, passwords, and multi-factor authentication.
3. Patch, vulnerability, and endpoint protection expectations.
4. Incident reporting, response, and recovery responsibilities.
5. Security awareness training and periodic control review.
