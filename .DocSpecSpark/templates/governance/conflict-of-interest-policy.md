# {{ORG_NAME}} Conflict of Interest Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how {{ORG_NAME}} identifies, discloses, and manages actual or perceived conflicts of interest.

## Policy Requirements

1. Covered persons disclose financial or personal interests promptly.
2. The board documents whether a conflict exists and the related facts.
3. Interested parties recuse from deliberation and voting.
4. Annual disclosure statements are collected and retained.
5. Violations are reviewed by leadership and documented in board records.
