# {{ORG_NAME}} Board Member Job Description

## Role Summary

Board members steward mission, strategy, and fiduciary oversight for {{ORG_NAME}}.

## Core Expectations

1. Attend board and committee meetings prepared to vote on key matters.
2. Uphold the duties of care, loyalty, and obedience.
3. Support fundraising, community advocacy, and executive oversight.
4. Complete conflict disclosures and protect confidential information.
5. Participate in annual board self-review and policy review.
