# {{ORG_NAME}} Document Retention and Destruction Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how {{ORG_NAME}} retains, protects, and disposes of governance, financial, HR, and program records.

## Policy Framework

1. Record categories are assigned retention periods and owners.
2. Legal holds suspend routine destruction when litigation or investigations arise.
3. Confidential records are stored and destroyed securely.
4. Electronic records and backups follow the same retention rules.
5. Destruction activity is authorized and documented.
