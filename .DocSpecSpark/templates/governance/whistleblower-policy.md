# {{ORG_NAME}} Whistleblower Policy

## Commitment

{{ORG_NAME}} encourages good-faith reporting of legal, financial, ethical, or safety concerns without retaliation.

## Reporting Framework

1. Team members and volunteers may report concerns to leadership or the board.
2. Reports are logged, reviewed, and investigated promptly.
3. Confidentiality is preserved to the extent practical.
4. Retaliation for good-faith reporting is prohibited.
5. Findings and corrective actions are documented and retained.
