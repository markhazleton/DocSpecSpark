# {{COMPANY_NAME}} Engineering Change Control Procedure

## Purpose

This procedure defines how {{COMPANY_NAME}} reviews, approves, and implements changes to drawings, BOMs, specifications, and process instructions.

## Procedure Outline

1. Change request intake and classification.
2. Cross-functional impact assessment and approvals.
3. Revision control for drawings, parts, and work instructions.
4. Implementation timing, communication, and verification.
5. Change record retention and post-change review.
