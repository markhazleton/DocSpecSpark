# {{ORG_NAME}} Volunteer Background Check Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines when {{ORG_NAME}} screens volunteers and how results are handled.

## Policy Requirements

1. Roles are classified by screening level and safeguarding risk.
2. Applicants receive notice and consent requirements before screening.
3. Results are reviewed confidentially by authorized staff.
4. Rescreening intervals and appeal steps are documented.
5. Screening decisions are retained with appropriate privacy controls.
