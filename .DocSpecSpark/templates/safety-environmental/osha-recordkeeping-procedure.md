# {{COMPANY_NAME}} OSHA Injury and Illness Recordkeeping Procedure

## Purpose

This procedure defines how {{COMPANY_NAME}} evaluates, records, and retains injury and illness records for covered operations.

## Procedure Outline

1. Recordable event criteria and reporting workflow.
2. OSHA 300, 300A, and 301 preparation responsibilities.
3. Posting, certification, and retention timelines.
4. Privacy case handling and employee access expectations.
5. Escalation path for severe or uncertain cases.
