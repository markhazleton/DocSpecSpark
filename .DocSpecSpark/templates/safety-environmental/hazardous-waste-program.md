# {{COMPANY_NAME}} Hazardous Waste Management Program

## Purpose

This program defines how {{COMPANY_NAME}} identifies, stores, ships, and documents hazardous waste.

## Program Outline

1. Waste identification and generator status determination.
2. Labeling, storage, and accumulation controls.
3. Manifest, shipment, and vendor coordination requirements.
4. Training, inspections, and contingency planning.
5. Record retention and regulatory reporting expectations.
