# {{COMPANY_NAME}} Severe Incident Reporting Procedure

## Purpose

This procedure defines how {{COMPANY_NAME}} reports fatalities and severe injuries within required timelines.

## Response Steps

1. Confirm whether the event meets OSHA reporting criteria.
2. Notify leadership, safety, legal, and site management immediately.
3. Preserve the scene and collect initial facts.
4. Complete external reporting within required timelines.
5. Document corrective actions and follow-up investigation tasks.
