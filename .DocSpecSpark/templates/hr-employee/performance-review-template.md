# {{COMPANY_NAME}} Performance Review Template

## Review Structure

Use this template to document employee performance, development areas, and next-period goals.

## Required Sections

1. Employee details, review period, and reviewer.
2. Performance against role expectations and goals.
3. Strengths, development needs, and coaching notes.
4. Goals and support plan for the next review cycle.
5. Employee comments, acknowledgments, and signatures.
