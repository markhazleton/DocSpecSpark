# {{COMPANY_NAME}} Separation Checklist

## Purpose

Use this checklist to coordinate operational, HR, IT, and security steps when a team member leaves {{COMPANY_NAME}}.

## Checklist Areas

1. Employment status, final day, and manager handoff.
2. Final pay, benefits, and required notices.
3. Return of company assets and records.
4. Account deactivation and confidentiality reminders.
5. Exit interview notes and completion sign-off.
