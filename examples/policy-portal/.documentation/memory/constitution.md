# Policy Portal Constitution

## Purpose
This repository is the source of truth for employee-facing and manager-facing operational documentation.

## Governance Rules
- Every controlled document must list an owner, approver, and annual review date.
- HR owns handbook content; IT owns security standards; Operations owns procedures.
- Policy changes that affect employee behavior require HR plus executive approval.
- Obsolete drafts and superseded policies move to archive paths instead of remaining in active folders.

## Multi-User Model
- Team-wide overrides live in `.documentation/commands/`.
- Personal workflow adjustments live in `.documentation/{git-user}/commands/`.
- Personal overrides may change wording and emphasis, but not bypass approval requirements.
