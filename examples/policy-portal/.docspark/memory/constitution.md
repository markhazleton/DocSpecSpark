# DocSpark Constitution

## Project
Policy Portal

## Purpose
Maintain operational policies, standards, and procedures through a document AI workflow derived from DevSpark.

## Core Principles
- Prefer a single source of truth for every policy topic.
- Require an explicit owner and review cadence for each controlled document.
- Treat ambiguity as a defect that must be resolved before publication.
- Archive superseded material so active guidance stays trustworthy.
